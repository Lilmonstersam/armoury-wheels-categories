ALL ALLOY WHEELS ARE NOT CREATED EQUAL

Experience peak performance and lasting durability with Armoury Group’s cutting-edge alloy truck wheels, meticulously crafted to deliver both style and substance. Explore our captivating range of finishes to make a bold statement on the road.

WHY DRIVERS ASK FOR ARMOURY

5-YEAR
WARRANTY

For your peace of mind, all Armoury forged alloy wheels feature a five-year warranty, with no mileage limit.

STANDARDS & CERTIFICATIONS

Our wheels carry American DOT, German TÜV and Japanese VIA product certification. Manufacturing certifications include IATF 16949 and ISO 14001.

CORROSION
RESISTANT

Our high-strength T6061 aluminium alloy has exceptional resistance to corrosion to stand the test of time.

UP TO 45% LIGHTER PER WHEEL

Armoury Wheels are nearly half the weight of conventional steel wheels, which delivers better handling, braking and steering performance.

EXTENDED LIFESPAN & DURABILITY

Increased service life of tyres and braking components, thanks to aluminium’s significantly superior heat dissipation compared to steel.

PERFORMANCE ENHANCING QUALITY

Armoury Wheels alloys have lower unsprung masses, with significant benefits on your acceleration, braking and cornering.
